14 year old me found photography fascinating after seeing someone documenting a wedding with a Canon 5D.That was when I realized that the beauty of this world could be captured and remembered for eternity. Since then, I’ve been in love with this hobby, and keep improving my skills untilone day i got into vocational school, i became the documentation team but most of the time i documenting by my self with schools canon 750D, documenting almost any event happening in 2023-2025, until in my 3rd grade.... i finaly saved up enough money buy myself a DSLR camera, its not that much, just a refurbish nikon D3000 with 30 year old lens, its very old camera, but im happy with it.
